How to Verify News Like a Professional Journalist

Four golden rules used by investigative newsrooms worldwide to fact-check breaking claims.

01

Trace to the Wire Service

Check if major independent news agencies like Reuters, Associated Press (AP), or Agence France-Presse (AFP) have published the same development.

02

Inspect Direct Quotations

Legitimate journalism explicitly quotes verifiable people by their full names, positions, and institutional affiliations rather than unnamed "sources".

03

Beware Emotional Clickbait

Disinformation often relies on intense emotional triggers like rage, urgency, ALL-CAPS words, and conspiracy tropes to spur shares before critical thought.

04

Check the Date and Primary Data

Misleading stories frequently take authentic photos or old headlines out of context from years prior to fabricate false breaking news narratives.
