VERITAS

The Challenge

Why Conventional Fake News Detectors Fail

Binary "True/False" is Broken

Real news isn't black-and-white. Most viral misinformation is "hybrid" — 70% genuine context wrapped around 30% fabricated or distorted claims. Users need to know how much is true.

Static Models Go Out of Date

A model trained only on historical text cannot verify breaking news happening right now. Without real-time news wire verification, models guess purely on writing style.

Web Scraping Friction

Users encounter articles across diverse websites like Times of India, NDTV, and international blogs. Systems must automatically scrape, parse, and isolate core claims cleanly.

System Pipeline

1

Algorithmic Foundation

Machine Learning & Stylometric Core

TF-IDF & Calibrated Logistic / Passive-Aggressive

Converts raw text into an n-gram feature space (1–2 tokens) with sublinear term-frequency scaling. A Calibrated Classifier produces continuous, well-calibrated posterior probabilities rather than brittle step-function decisions.

X = TfidfVectorizer(ngram=(1,2), sublinear_tf=True)
clf = CalibratedClassifierCV(LogisticRegression())

Stylometric & Sensationalism Forensics

Quantifies linguistic red flags that machine learning alone might miss:

  • Sensationalism Score: "SHOCKING", "MIRACLE", "EXPOSED"
  • Capitalization Ratio: Excessive ALL-CAPS words
  • Punctuation Surge: Multiple exclamation and question marks
  • Attribution Index: "according to", "spokesperson stated"
Live Corroboration

Live News Cross-Referencing & Debunk Matching

Atomic Claim Splitting

Segments long articles into discrete assertions. Each claim is evaluated individually rather than scoring the article as an inseparable blob.

Zero-Key News Search

Queries Google News RSS dynamically for contemporaneous coverage across NDTV, Times of India, The Hindu, and Reuters.

Debunk Signature Registry

Instant matching against known viral scams (GPS in 2000 note, UNESCO anthem, lemon cancer cure) citing official debunks.

Allows Veritas to verify breaking news in real time without waiting for retraining or paying expensive third-party API fees!
Veracity Metric Formulation

How Truth Percentage is Calculated

Truth % = (0.35 × Claims_Ratio) + (0.30 × ML_Prob) + (0.20 × Domain_Trust) + (0.15 × Live_Corroboration)
35% Claims Truth Ratio

Mean truth score across all extracted atomic factual statements.

30% ML Inference

N-gram text stylometry and deception probability.

20% Domain Reputation

Pre-indexed authority ratings for NDTV, TOI, BBC vs unverified domains.

15% Live Corroboration

Presence of independent media reports confirming or debunking.

Critical Debunk Override: If any single claim matches an accredited hoax or fact-checker debunk, the score is capped at ≤ 25%.
